The power of the letter

I was invited to organize an exhibition and decided to focus on calligraphy and music. The idea was to create sheets where the calligraphy conveyed the power of music. Part of it was professional work, but I also wanted to have fun experimenting and combining different techniques. Within a couple of months, we developed a full theme of music-related sheets, and for the opening, we held a live calligraphy and music demonstration. We also organized a calligraphy workshop, and on Mother’s Day, I was at the café to collect commissions and requests.
