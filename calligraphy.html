The art of imperfection

Calligraphy has something special. When you start, it’s frustrating because you’re searching for perfection. But when you truly start enjoying it, imperfection becomes beautiful.

The power of calligraphy lies in pure expression through its strokes. Just like typography, different styles of letters can evoke different feelings.

The power of the letter

In 2018 I held a calligraphy exhibition at El Bullidor (Rubí). For the opening, we organized a live concert and demonstration. Besides creating the artworks, I took care of all the exhibition management, including posters, cards, and other communication materials.

Besides calligraphy, I’m a bit of a geek ;)

I’ve always loved Uncial calligraphy, either due to my Celtic roots or my fascination with The Lord of the Rings. With my obsession for the Game of Thrones book series, I started creating a map of all of Westeros.

Later, I used a class project to further experiment with different materials—inks, watercolors, acrylics, and papers. Although it’s not perfect work, it was a great way to experiment, and I really enjoyed it.
