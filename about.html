I was born in Ponferrada, in northern León near Galicia. Always curious to see the world, I moved to Oviedo to study, where my path in design began.

Later I settled in Barcelona, where I spent over a decade living, learning and enjoying Mediterranean life. Now I’m back in Ponferrada, surrounded by nature and still connected to projects and people from many places.
