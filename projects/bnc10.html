We’ve been working together for a year now, developing new features and improving existing ones.

A year and a half ago I heard about this neobank, a very interesting and innovative project born in Barcelona. After some time, I started collaborating with them — first improving certain functionalities and later working on new projects. It’s a constantly changing sector, which means the challenges are ongoing. I love their philosophy as a bank — far from traditional bureaucracy: transparent, ethical, and not playing with users’ money. Haven’t you heard of them yet? bnc10

Design system

We’ve been working on redesigning the app and creating its design system. This is what I can show — there’s much more progress behind the scenes ;)

New design

Deposit money

This was the first feature we worked on — two flows: one for copying or sharing bank details to make a transfer, and another to deposit money using a credit card from another bank.

* These flows have already been updated but are not yet implemented.

Apple Pay / Google Pay

Mobile payment is becoming increasingly relevant, even though we’re still catching up with other countries. It also stands out as a more sustainable option.

Usage flow

The activation of both Apple Pay and Google Pay depends on an internal platform where the user validates and accepts the terms. We had to design and get it approved before launch. The design process was quicker — aside from copy and image review.

The mobile payment revolution

Pay online quickly and securely — no need to type in banking details. Forgetting your wallet at home will never be a problem again.

Phone with NFC

You need a phone with NFC technology to pay contactless.

App installed

You can add your virtual card to Apple Wallet or Google Pay.

Stores and online

Pay in stores with the symbol or by clicking the Apple Pay or Google Pay button.

Shared accounts

We’re working on several projects at the moment, and new features will be released soon. This was the latest addition: a shared account for two people, to make managing common expenses easier.

A neobank for everyday life

Manage accounts quickly and easily — a person using their bnc10 account, both personal and shared.
