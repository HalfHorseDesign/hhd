NETIP Dental

Web design
Client
NETIP Dental
Year
2019
Sector
Healthcare · Dental
Website
Official site
Developer
Fred Russias
Owner
Armando Vasquez

It was important to be up-to-date, so the site needed a facelift, renewing the design and structuring all the information.

When Armando contacted me to redesign his website, I felt a little overwhelmed. I didn’t know anything about web, didn’t understand most terms, and wasn’t familiar with the sector. His company sells technology to dental clinics—both products and services—covering all IT needs of dentists, from infrastructure, systems, and remote administration to security for sensitive patient data collection.

Previous style

New design

Information architecture

First, I organized all the content because there was a problem with its structure. I started structuring the information, dealing with technical jargon and language complexity.

User flow

Work process

Research

Research is conducted and the experience is designed.

UI design

A look & feel proposal is prepared and all screens are designed.

Interaction

A series of animations are designed.

Coordination with the developer

Responsive layout grid

Following the 8pt system, I worked with 12 columns, thinking about adaptation to other devices. All elements individually and in relation to each other follow the 8pt rule, allowing for more coordinated work.

Resources search

The provided graphic resources were very limited and often low-quality. The rebranding made me find icons, illustrations, and photos—even using some of my own photos.

Coordination with developers

I followed an agile methodology to manage delivery phases with the development team. I used Trello for organization, Zeplin to facilitate the work, and sent them videos of the animations made with Flinto.
